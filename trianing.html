ABOUT

What is Linux?

Linux is an open-source family of operating systems built around the Linux kernel. Ubuntu is a popular Linux distribution maintained by Canonical and a large open-source community.

The command line gives you direct control over files, processes, networking, packages, permissions, services and system configuration.

Why learn the terminal?

  • Automate repetitive tasks with shell scripts.
  • Manage servers remotely through SSH.
  • Understand permissions, processes and networking.
  • Work effectively with development and DevOps tools.

Safety rule

Never run a command you do not understand on a production system. Commands involving sudo, deletion, disks, permissions and networking can change or destroy data.
